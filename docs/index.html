Why Bench & Bar

Two scores that actually matter on the road

Star ratings don’t tell you if the gym has a squat rack or the bar makes a real Negroni. We do.

Bar & gym, rated

Every hotel scored 1–5 on the bar and the gym, plus an overall — the things that make or break a work trip.

⚡

Rate in 3 taps

Tappable rows of icons. Tap the 4th barbell = 4/5. No typing, under 30 seconds, notes always optional.

📍

Near-me search + filters

Defaults to “near me,” then filter by bar/gym score, tags like “squat rack” or “open late,” hotel brand, price, and distance.

🏷️

Check prices & book

Pick your dates, see the best price across partners, and book in a tap — right from the hotel.

👥

Follow travelers

A feed of reviews from people you follow, public profiles, and “people you follow rated this” on every hotel.

🏆

Badges & leaderboards

Climb contributor tiers, earn badges, and see the top-rated hotels and top reviewers.
